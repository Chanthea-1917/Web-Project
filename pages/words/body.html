Wake up : To stop sleeping.

Sleep : To rest at night with eyes closed.

Eat : To take food into the mouth and swallow.

Drink : To take liquid into the mouth and swallow.

Walk : To move quickly with your feet.

Run : To move quickly with your feet.

Sit : To rest on a chair or the ground.

Stand : To be upright on your feet.

Read : To look at and understand written words.

Write : To put words or letters on paper.

Open : To move something so it is not closed.

Close : To move something so it is shut.

Pay : To enjoy yourself, often with games or toys.

Work : To do a job or activity for results or money.

Study : To learn by reading or practicing.

Cook : To prepare food by heating.

Wash : To clean something with water..

Clean : To remove dirt from something.

Buy : To get something by paying money.

Sell : To give something to someone for money.

Market : A place where people buy and sell goods.

Food : Things we eat to live.

Water : Clear liquid that we drink and need to live.

Rice : Small white or brown grains we eat as food.

Fruit : Sweet food from plants (apple, banana, etc.).

Car : A vehicle with four wheels for driving.

Bus : A large vehicle that carries many people.

Clothes : Things we wear on our body.

Shoes : Things we wear on our feet.

Phone : A device for talking to people or sending messages.

Friend : A person you like and spend time with.

Teacher : A person who teaches students.

Good : Something nice, correct, or useful.

Bad : Not good, unpleasant.
