Sweet : Tastes like sugar or honey.

Sour : Tastes sharp, like lemon or vinegar.

Salty : Tastes of salt.

Bitter : Tastes sharp and unpleasant, like black coffee.

Spicy : Tastes hot, like chili or pepper.

Savory : Tastes rich and delicious, like cooked meat or soup.

Umami : A deep, meaty flavor often found in mushrooms, soy sauce, or meat.

Bland : Tastes plain, no strong flavor.

Tangy : Tastes sharp but pleasant, like orange or tamarind.

Smoky : Tastes like food cooked on fire or smoke.

butter : Tastes rich and soft, like butter.

Minty : Cool and fresh, like mint leaves.

Fruity : Tastes like fruit.

Nutty : Tastes like nuts (almonds, peanuts, etc.).

Herbal : Tastes like herbs or medicine plants.

Peppery : Sharp taste, like black pepper.

Garlicky : Strong taste of garlic.

Gingery : Warm, spicy taste of ginger.

Oily : Tastes like too much oil or fat.

Vinegary : Sour taste like vinegar.

Citrusy : Fresh taste like lemon, lime, or orange.

Zesty : Fresh, lively taste with a little sourness.

Pungent : Very strong taste or smell (like onion or garlic).

Malty : Sweet, grainy taste like malt or roasted grain.

Caramelized : Sweet, like sugar cooked until brown.

Syrupy : Very sweet, thick like syrup.

Chocolatey : Tastes like chocolate.

honeyed : Sweet taste like honey.

Floral : Smells or tastes like flowers.

Gemey : Strong taste of wild meat.

Eggy : Tastes or smells like eggs.

Crispy : Crunchy texture when bitten.
