Family : A group of people that live together in one house.

Father : A male parent.

Mother : A female parent.

Parents : The father and mother together.

Son :A male child of parents.

Daughter : A female child of parents.

Child : A young boy or girl.

Children : More than one child.

Brother : A male sibling.

Sister :A female sibling.

Sibling :A brother or sister.

Grandfather : The father of your father or mother.

Grandmother : The mother of your father or mother.

Grandparents : The parents of your parents.

Uncle : The brother of your father or mother.

Aunt : The sister of your father or mother.

Cusion : The child of your uncle or aunt.

Nephew : The son of your brother or sister.

Nice : The daughter of your brother or sister.

Husband : A married man.

Wife : A married woman.

Stepfather : The husband of your mother, but not your real father.

Stepmother : The wife of your father, but not your real mother.

Stepbrother : The son of your stepmother or stepfather.

Stepsister : The daughter of your stepmother or stepfather.

Half-brother :A brother who shares only one parent with you.

Half-sister : A sister who shares only one parent with you.

Twins : Two children born at the same time to the same mother.

Baby : A very young child.

Toddler : A small child who is just learning to walk.

Teenager : A young person between 13 and 19 years old.

Adult :A fully grown person.

Relative : A person who belongs to the same family.

Ancestor : A family member who lived long ago.

Descendant :A family member born after another.

Generation : All family members born around the same time.

Marriage : The legal union of husband and wife.

Divorce : The legal ending of a marriage.

Engagement : A married womanAn agreement to marry in the future.

Bride : A married A woman about to get married.

Groom : A man about to get married.

Couple : Two people in a romantic relationship or marriage.

Guardian : A person legally responsible for a child.

Orphan :A person legally responsible for a child.

Household : All the people living in one house.
