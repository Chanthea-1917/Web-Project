Definition

School : A place where students go to study and learn.

Teacher:A person who teaches students in a school.

Student:A person who studies at school.

Classroom:A room where lessons are taugh.

Priciple:The head or leader of a school.

Book:A set of written or printed pages bound together.

Pencil:A tool used for writing or drawing with lead.

Pen:A tool for writing with ink.

Eraser:A small object used to remove pencil marks.

Ruler:A straight tool used to measure length.

Bag:Smething used to carry books and school supplies.

Blackboard:A large board for writing with chalk.

Chalk:A white stick used for writing on a blackboard.

Desk:A piece of furniture where students sit and study.

Chair:A seat with four legs for sitting.

Library:A place where books are kept for reading or borrowing.

Playground:An outdoor area where children play.

Canteen:A place in school where food and drinks are sold.

Homework:Schoolwork that students do at home.

Test:A short exam to check students' knowledge.

Uniform:Special clothes that students wear at school.

Subject:An area of study, like math, science, or English.

Lesson:A period of learning taught by a teacher.

Schedual:A plan that shows times for lessons or activities.

Friend:A someone you like and spend time with.

Study:To spend time learning about a subject, usually from books or teachers.

Teach:To help someone learn by giving lessons or showing how to do something.

Read:To look at and understand written or printed words.

Write:To use a pen, pencil, or keyboard to put words or symbols on paper or a screen.

Draw:To make a picture using a pen, pencil, or crayons.

Listen:To pay attention to sounds or what someone is saying.

Speak:To use words with your voice to communicate.

Play:To do activities for fun or to take part in a game or sport.

Count:To say numbers in order or find the total of something.

Ask:To say something to get an answer or information.

Answer:To give a reply to a question.
