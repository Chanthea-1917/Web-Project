The boy who could speak to the star

Evan lived in a village where the night sky was always clear and full of stars. Ever since he was a child, he had felt a strange connection to the heavens, as if the stars were trying to tell him something. One quiet night, while lying on a hill, he whispered, “Hello,” and to his surprise, a soft voice replied from the sky. The stars spoke of distant galaxies, forgotten worlds, and ancient stories. They taught Evan about courage, curiosity, and the importance of asking questions. He shared their wisdom with the villagers, helping them solve problems, understand nature, and appreciate the universe around them. But one night, the stars warned him of a coming darkness — a cold shadow that would sweep across the land if people forgot the lessons of the sky. Evan worked tirelessly, teaching children, elders, and neighbors to cherish knowledge, wonder, and kindness. Years passed, and Evan grew old, but the night sky remained bright and full of secrets. He realized that the stars weren’t just distant lights — they were reminders that everyone, no matter how small, can shine and guide others. And every time the villagers looked up, they remembered the boy who listened to the stars.
