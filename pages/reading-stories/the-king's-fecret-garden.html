The king's secret garden

Long ago, there was a small fishing village by the sea. Everyone in the village had heard stories about a hidden treasure buried deep within an old island that lay across the horizon. The island was covered with thick jungle, tall cliffs, and dangerous caves. Most villagers were too afraid to go there. But one boy named Ravi was different. He was curious, brave, and full of dreams. Every night, his grandmother told him tales about pirates who once sailed across the sea and buried their gold on that mysterious island. “One day,” she whispered, “a pure-hearted person will find it.” One summer morning, Ravi built a small raft from bamboo and set sail alone. The sea was calm, but the journey was long. After hours of paddling, he reached the island. The place was wild and strange—birds with bright feathers sang in the trees, and vines hung everywhere like ropes. Ravi walked deeper and deeper into the jungle. He carried only a lantern, a map his grandmother had drawn, and his courage. He found dark caves, old stone carvings, and even skeletons of long-dead pirates. Finally, he reached a spot marked with a big X on the map. With trembling hands, he dug into the earth. Suddenly, his shovel hit something hard—a chest! He pulled it out, wiped off the dirt, and opened it slowly. Inside, he expected to see gold and jewels. But instead, he found… books. Old, leather-bound books, filled with maps, knowledge, and wisdom of the past. At first, Ravi felt disappointed. But as he turned the pages, he realized this treasure was even greater than gold. The books contained cures for illnesses, farming techniques, star maps for safe sailing, and stories of ancient times. When Ravi returned to the village, he shared the knowledge with everyone. Soon, the village became rich—not with money, but with ideas, inventions, and wisdom. The villagers built better boats, grew more food, and healed the sick.
