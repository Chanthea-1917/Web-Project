Present simple

Usage : We use present simple to talk about general truths, habits, routines, unchanging situations, and fixed future events.

Form

Positive: Subject + V1 + Object

Exaple: I go to school.

Negative: Subject + do/does + not + V1 + Object

Example: She doesn't eat the mango.

Question: Do/Does + subject + V1 + Object

Example: Do you buy the ball?

I/You/We/They

go to the market

He/She/It

goes to the market

I/You/We/They

don't go to the market

He/She/It

doesn't go to the market

  • Add -es: for verb ending o, s, x, ch, sh
  • Change -y to ies: for verbs ending in a consonant followed by -y.
  • Add -s: for most verbs, simply add -s to the base form.
