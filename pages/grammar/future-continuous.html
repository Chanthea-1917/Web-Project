Future continuous

Usage : We use the present perfect to talk about an action or situation that started in the past and continues to the present

Form

Positive: Subject + have/has + V3 + Object.

Exaple: I have leant English since 2015.

Negative: Subject + have/has + not + V3 + Object

Example: They have played football.

Question: Have/Has + subject + V3 + Object

Example: Has she tried to study?

I/You/We/They

have learnt English

He/She/It

has learnt English

I/You/We/They
