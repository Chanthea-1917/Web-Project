Present continous

Usage :We use present continuous to talk about actions happening right now, temporary situations or habits, future plans and arrangements, and trends or changes over time.

Form

Positive: Subject + tobe + V(ing) + Object.

Exaple: She is cooking food.

Negative: Subject + tobe + not + V(ing) + Object.

Example: They are playing baskateball.

Question: Tobe + Subject + V(ing) + Object?

Example: Are you learning English?

  • Add -double consonant: for verb ending consonant-vowel-consonant.

    • Examlpe: Sit >> Sitting

    Cut >> Cutting

    Stop >> Stopping

  • Change -e to ing: for verbs ending in a vowel -e delete e add ing.

    Examlpe: Write >> Writing

    Delete >> Deleting

    Stop >> Stopping

  • vowel-y we don't add the double consonant.

    Examlpe: Play >> Playing

    Stay >> Staying

    Buy >> Buying

    • We use time maker to signal actions happening at the moment of speaking

    Now/Right now/At the moment/Today/This week/These day/while

    Examlpe:

    He is looking after his girlfriend today.

    Now, they are watching movie.

    Are you taking a shower right now?
