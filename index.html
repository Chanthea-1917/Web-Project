Grammar

Tense Definition/Function Example
Present simple Habits or do it again and again She eats rice.
Present continuous Things are happening now You are learning English.
Present perfect Things in the past but are still true now She have taken a shower.
Present perfect continuous Things that happened in the past and still continuing in the present He has been learning English.
Past simple Things that happened in the past and completed in the past They climbed the palm tree.
Past continuous Things that ongoing for a period of time in the past and interrupt by another past even While we were playing our mum arrived home.
Past perfect The past action that happened before anoter action in the past After I'd gone to the store, I came home.
Past perfect continuous To describe an action that was ongoing up to a certain point in the past She had been studying when the power went out.
Simple futrue To talk about things you believe or expect will happen They will go home.
Future continuous An action will be in progress at a specific time in the future This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Australia.
Future perfect To talk about an action that will be completed before a specific point in the future By 5 p.m., the conference will have finished.
Futrue perfect continuou To emphasise the continuous nature of an action By 3:00, they will have been studying for four hours
